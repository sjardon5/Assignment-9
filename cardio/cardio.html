Cardio Exercises

Cardio exercise uses continuous movement to raise your heart rate and improve your overall fitness. It can help with weight loss, stamina, heart health, and energy.

Choose activities you enjoy so you can keep a regular routine. Walking, jogging, cycling, dancing, and swimming are all excellent options.
