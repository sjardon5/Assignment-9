Vipassana

Vipassana is a mindfulness practice focused on observing the body and mind without judgment. It can help reduce stress, improve emotional awareness, and increase calmness.

Regular practice encourages a deeper awareness of thoughts and sensations, making it easier to respond to stress more peacefully.
