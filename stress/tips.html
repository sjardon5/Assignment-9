5 Helpful Tips

  1. Take short breaks during the day to reset your mind.
  2. Exercise regularly to reduce tension and improve mood.
  3. Get enough sleep to help your body recover.
  4. Practice breathing exercises or meditation.
  5. Focus on one task at a time and ask for support when needed.
